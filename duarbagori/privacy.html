Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how the Duarbagori Reports Android app ("the app") handles your information. The app has no server of its own and does not collect your data for its developer.

Information kept on your phone

Your complaints, their location, notes, photos, videos, attached files and your settings (name, phone, email) are stored only on your phone. You can delete any complaint inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Location, camera and microphone

The app uses your location to record where the problem is and to find the responsible offices, and the camera and microphone to take evidence photos and videos. These are used only when you are making a report and are never sent anywhere except inside the complaints you choose to send.

Google account data

If you choose "Continue with Google", the app requests two permissions:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): used only to send the complaints you create, to the government offices you select, when you press Send. The app cannot read, search, delete or change your emails.
  • See your email address (userinfo.email): used to show which account complaints are sent from.

Your Google access is handled by Google on your phone. The app does not store your Google password, does not keep a copy of the emails it sends, and does not send your Google data to the developer or anyone else. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections or by signing out in the app's settings.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Other email accounts

If you set up a non-Google email account instead, its password is stored encrypted on your phone using the Android Keystore and is used only to send your complaints.

SMS

If you allow it, the app sends a short text about your complaint from your own SIM to the selected offices' mobile numbers. Your operator's normal SMS charges apply.

Sharing

Information leaves your phone only inside the complaints you send, and only to the offices you select. The developer receives none of it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
