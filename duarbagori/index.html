Duarbagori Reports

Duarbagori Reports is an Android app that helps citizens in India report civic problems and illegal activities, such as potholes, garbage, illegal sand mining, pollution, encroachment, illegal liquor and more, to the government offices legally responsible for them.

What the app does

  • Records the exact location of the problem and lets you add photos, video and any other files as evidence.
  • Finds the responsible department for the type of problem and place, from the local body and district up to the state, the central government and the courts.
  • Sends your complaint by email from your own Gmail account, and by SMS from your own SIM, when you press Send.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", the app asks Google for permission only to send email on your behalf and to see your email address. It cannot read, delete or change any of your emails. It sends only the complaints you choose to send.
