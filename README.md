# Duarbagori Reports

Android app (Kotlin / Jetpack Compose) to photograph, geotag and report civic and environmental problems to every responsible office, from the local body up to the Prime Minister's Office and the Supreme Court.

## What it does
- 14 problem types: pothole, deforestation, illegal sand mining, illegal construction, garbage dumping, waterlogging, street light, encroachment, noise, water pollution, air pollution, wildlife crime, drinking water, bribery/corruption.
- Photo and video capture with GPS geotags, map pin refinement.
- **Attach any file, any type, any size** (PDF, audio, long video, documents). Files are referenced, not copied. They go into the email, or use "Share via any app" for files too large for mail.
- Routes each complaint by department and level: Local body / Autonomous Council → District (DC, SP) → State (PWD, Forest, Mines, Pollution Board, Police, Vigilance, Lokayukta, CM, Chief Secretary) → Central (ministries, CPCB, WCCB, CVC, CPGRAMS, PMO) → Courts (High Court, NGT, Supreme Court; unticked by default).
- Add any office yourself if it is missing.

## Contact directory
`app/src/main/assets/authorities.json`, generated by `tools/gen_authorities.py`. Every contact was taken from official sites (or the official CPCB directory) on 2026-11-15; the `source` field says where.
Full department coverage: Assam, with district offices for Karbi Anglong (incl. KAAC), West Karbi Anglong, Golaghat, Jorhat, Dima Hasao and SPs for other Assam districts.
Other states: Chief Minister's grievance line and State Pollution Control Board, plus all national offices.
